How to play

5 rounds. 7 lives. One set of milestones a day.
  • Drag the 5 tiles onto the rail in chronological order, earliest at the top.
  • Tap Check Order when all 5 slots are filled.
  • A wrong order costs 1 life — you can keep adjusting and retry.
  • Run out of 7 lives and your run ends for the day.
  • Solve all 5 rounds to win. Difficulty rises each round, and the category never repeats from the day before.
  • When you finish (or run out of lives), you can review every timeline you solved that session.
  • Use Share Results to copy a spoiler-free emoji recap, themed to your last solved category.
  • Missed a day? Catch up anytime in the Archive.
